Privacy Policy: DreamyDoo Kids Uploader

Last updated: 28 October 2026

The DreamyDoo Kids Uploader (“the app”) is a private tool that publishes episodes to the DreamyDoo Kids YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner. This policy explains what the app does with Google and YouTube data.

Who uses the app

Only the owner of the DreamyDoo Kids channel. The app is not offered to the public, and no other person signs in to it.

What Google data the app accesses, and why

With the channel owner's permission, through the official YouTube Data API, the app:

  • uploads the channel's own videos, sets their titles, descriptions, tags, captions and thumbnails, and schedules when they are published;
  • reads the channel's own list of uploaded videos and their processing and privacy status, to confirm an upload succeeded and to avoid uploading the same episode twice;
  • changes the publication schedule of, or deletes, the channel's own videos (for example, a test video or an episode the owner rejects).

It requests the permissions youtube.upload and youtube.force-ssl for these purposes only.

What is stored

  • An OAuth authorization token for the owner's channel, stored as an encrypted secret in a private GitHub repository and used only by the app's automated jobs. It is never published or shared.
  • A small private record of the channel's own episodes: episode identifiers, YouTube video IDs, titles, publication times and upload status.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or process any information about viewers, subscribers or commenters.
  • It does not read comments, messages, analytics, or any account other than the DreamyDoo Kids channel.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.
  • It does not use Google user data to train AI or machine-learning models.

Children's privacy

DreamyDoo Kids makes content for young children, and every video is marked as made for kids on YouTube. The app collects no personal information from children or anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are made under the YouTube Terms of Service and are covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token then stops working immediately, and the owner deletes the stored secret.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new date. For questions, contact the channel through its YouTube About page.
