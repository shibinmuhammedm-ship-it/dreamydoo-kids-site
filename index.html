About this site

DreamyDoo Kids is a YouTube channel of short, original animated stories about counting, colors, shapes, kindness, sharing and nature, starring Ellie, Dodo the puppy and their animal friends.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@DreamyDooKids-1

DreamyDoo Kids Uploader

The DreamyDoo Kids Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner. It uses the official YouTube Data API to upload the channel's own episodes to the DreamyDoo Kids channel, schedule their publication, add captions and thumbnails, and check that each upload succeeded.

  • It is not offered to the public and does not let anyone else sign in.
  • It does not collect, store or share any information about viewers.
  • Every video is marked as made for kids.
